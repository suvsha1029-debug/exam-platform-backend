Process

Controlled Onboarding

1. Request
Institute submits official details and requested service scope.
2. Platform Review
Platform team verifies institute identity and service suitability.
3. People Mapping
After approval, institute nominates responsible people with designation and contact details.
4. Secure Access
Approved people receive password setup links. Passwords are never emailed or viewed by operators.

All requests are logged. Platform approval is required before any admin, master-admin, or question-generation access is enabled.
